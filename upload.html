Presentation Formats

ForestSAT 2026 offers four presentation formats, reflecting its hybrid format:

🏛️ In-Person Presentations
  • Oral Presentation (in person): 12-minute talk followed by a 3-minute Q&A session, delivered live at the University of Florida. Authors are required to submit their presentation slides (PPTX) using the conference template (PPTX) . Presentations must be submitted via the portal by April 27, 2026. This will allow our team to ensure smooth delivery of content to all in-person and virtual participants.

  • Poster Presentation (in person): Posters will be displayed in the Grand Ballroom for the duration of the conference. Authors are requested to be present at their poster during the Poster Reception on Tuesday, May 5, 2026, from 5:00 PM to 7:00 PM.

Poster Display Specifications

  • One side of a poster board will be provided, along with pushpins.
  • Display area: 60 in (152 cm) height × 72 in (182 cm) width.
  • Total board height (including pedestal): 74 in (187 cm).
  • Setup: Monday, May 4, 2026, from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM or Tuesday, May 5, 2026, from 7:30 AM to 8:30 AM
  • Removal: Thursday, May 7, 2026, from 3:30 PM to 5:00 PM

Several local businesses can print posters for reasonable rates (less than $40 USD). Please contact them directly:

🌐 Virtual Presentations
  • Oral Presentation (virtual): 12-minute talk followed by a 3-minute Q&A session, delivered via Zoom. Authors presenting virtually will do so in real time during their assigned session and timeslot. Please note that the conference time zone is U.S. Eastern Time. Authors are required to submit their presentation slides (PPTX) using the conference template (PPTX) and a 12-minute recorded presentation to be used as a backup in case of connectivity issues, using the portal below. Presentations must be submitted via the portal by April 27, 2026. This will allow our team to ensure smooth delivery of content to all in-person and virtual participants.

  • Poster Presentation (virtual): Virtual poster presentations consist of a 5-minute presentation followed by a 2-minute Q&A session, delivered via Zoom. Authors presenting virtually will do so in real time during the virtual poster session timeslot (see Agenda for details). Please note that the conference time zone is U.S. Eastern Time. Authors are required to submit their presentation slides (PPTX) and a 5-minute recorded presentation to be used as a backup in case of connectivity issues. Please use the portal below.

Video Format Guidelines

To ensure compatibility and smooth playback across all sessions, please follow the guidelines below:

  • File format: MP4 (recommended)
  • Maximum duration:
    • Oral presentations: 12 minutes
    • Poster presentations: 5 minutes
  • Resolution: 1080p (1920 × 1080) recommended
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9
  • Audio: Clear narration (built-in microphone or headset is acceptable)
  • File size: Keep under 300 MB whenever possible

Submission Deadline: All presentation materials must be submitted via the portal by April 27, 2026.

Code of Conduct

We are dedicated to providing a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion (or lack thereof), or technology choices. We do not tolerate harassment of participants in any form. Suggestive statements, sexual innuendo, or offensive remarks are not appropriate during any activity, including during talks, poster sessions, workshops, social functions, after hours parties, via Zoom chat or on Twitter or other online media. Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately. Those violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled without a refund at the discretion of the organizers. If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns about participant behavior, please notify the organizers immediately.

Hotel Reservation Fraud Alert

It is possible you may be contacted by a company claiming to be the official housing bureau for this conference. These companies contact conference exhibitors and attendees, telling them the guest room block is sold out at the host hotel. The company then quotes a rate at another hotel, claiming it is an official room block and that you must reserve through them to get discounted rates. They ask for your credit card information so they can make a reservation for you. DO NOT FALL FOR THIS or you will lose your money. This scam is affecting conferences all over the country. No matter what meeting you attend, always use the hotel reservation links provided on the host organization’s website.
