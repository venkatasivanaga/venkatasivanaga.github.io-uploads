Presentation Formats

ForestSAT 2026 offers four presentation formats, reflecting its hybrid format.

🏛️ In-Person Presentations
  • Oral Presentation (in person): 12-minute talk followed by a 3-minute Q&A session, delivered live at the University of Florida.
  • Poster Presentation (in person): Posters will be displayed in the Grand Ballroom for the duration of the conference.
🌐 Virtual Presentations
  • Oral Presentation (virtual): submit slides and a 12-minute backup recording.
  • Poster Presentation (virtual): submit slides and a 5-minute backup recording.

Video Format Guidelines

  • File format: MP4 recommended
  • Resolution: 1080p recommended
  • Aspect ratio: 16:9
  • Audio: Clear narration
  • File size: Video 300 MB max, Presentation 100 MB max
